# SomaSense for iPhone

SomaSense is a free and open source calendar tool for logging symptoms and sensations that are felt across the body. All data entered into the app is stored on your iPhone without being transmitted to the developer or to Apple. You may share your own calendar log by email or to other apps on your iPhone by selecting the Share option in the Calendar or Reports tab.

Use SomaSense to track feelings you have across the body over time and to monitor their progression or transience. When noticing any new or unusual pain or symptom, the tool can be used to create a log of a symptom’s progression for sharing with a physician. Please seek immediate care if you have a new symptom you haven’t addressed with your healthcare provider previously.

## How it works

- **Body map:** tap where you feel something on a 3D body, or press and hold, then drag, to paint an area. Turn the body to mark the front, back or sides, and zoom in to mark fingers or toes.
- **Reports:** describe each symptom with its type, severity, notes, medicines, physical activity and concerns.
- **Calendar:** see which days had symptoms, colored by the highest severity, and mark days when you had none.
- **Reports:** see how often each type of symptom happened over the last 7, 30 or 90 days, where on the body, at what time of day, and what was logged alongside it.
- **Sharing:** email a month's log or a report as a PDF, for example to your clinician, or send it to another app.

SomaSense is designed to work with VoiceOver and larger text sizes, and needs no account or internet connection.

## Body shape

To customize body shape model to your body type, you may make modifications in the Settings tab of the app.
