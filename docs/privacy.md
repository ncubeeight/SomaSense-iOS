# SomaSense for iPhone Privacy Policy

**Effective November 12, 2026**

SomaSense is a free and open source calendar tool from ncubeeight LLC for logging symptoms and sensations felt across the body. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the SomaSense app for iPhone. In short, your log stays on your iPhone unless you choose to share it, and neither ncubeeight LLC nor Apple receives it from SomaSense.

## What the app stores on your iPhone

SomaSense saves the following in its private storage on your iPhone:

- Your reports: date, time, symptom or sensation types, body locations, severity, descriptions and notes, medicines, physical activity, other sensations and concerns
- The days you mark as symptom-free
- Your settings: body shape and any symptom types, medicines, activities or sensations you remove from the lists of choices

This can include health information, so please keep it in mind when you decide who can use your iPhone. SomaSense doesn't read from or write to the Health app.

## What ncubeeight LLC collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet. It asks for no permissions, so it can't read your location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts or files. ncubeeight LLC has no copy of your log and can't see, recover or delete it for you.

If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's Analytics & Improvements settings, Apple may give ncubeeight LLC crash reports and usage statistics for the app. Apple handles these under its own privacy policy, and they don't include the contents of your log.

## When you share your log

The Share buttons on the Calendar and Reports tabs send your information only when you choose to. Before anything is sent, the app shows a preview of the PDF. A Calendar share includes a calendar picture, a PDF and a text list of that month's entries. A Reports share includes a PDF and a text summary for the period you selected.

Email opens a message in Apple's Mail app with the files attached and the text in the message, for you to address and send. Share opens the iOS share sheet, where you pick the app, such as another email app, Messages or Save to Files, and the recipient.

Once shared, the information is handled by the app and the people you sent it to, under their own privacy practices. Sending it by email or chat also means it travels over the internet through those services. SomaSense can't recall anything you've already shared.

To prepare a share, the app keeps a temporary copy of the files on your iPhone until your next share, until you delete your data, or until iOS clears temporary files. Save to Files puts the files in the location you choose, where they stay until you remove them. Delete stored on-device data doesn't remove files you saved this way.

## Backups and new iPhones

SomaSense leaves its data out of iCloud Backup and computer backups, so your log isn't copied to iCloud and won't move to a new iPhone. To keep a copy, share your log before changing phones.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your iPhone until you remove it. You can delete a single report from its detail screen, or select Delete stored on-device data in Settings to remove everything. Deleting the app also removes it. Deleted data can't be restored.

## Security

Your log is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. iOS encrypts it whenever your iPhone is locked. SomaSense doesn't have its own passcode, so anyone who can use your unlocked iPhone can open it. Please use a passcode.

## Other companies

Companies that provide your iPhone and its software have their own privacy practices that SomaSense doesn't control. These include Apple, for the App Store, iOS and Mail, and your keyboard app, which handles what you type.

Links in the app open in your web browser. This website is hosted by GitHub and loads fonts from Google, so those companies may receive your IP address and browser details when you visit. ncubeeight LLC doesn't add analytics to this website.

## Children

SomaSense is not directed to children under 13, and ncubeeight LLC does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date. Past versions are available in the project's public history on GitHub.

## Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at [the SomaSense for iPhone GitHub repository](https://github.com/ncubeeight/SomaSense-iOS/issues). Issues are public, so please don't include health details or other personal information.
